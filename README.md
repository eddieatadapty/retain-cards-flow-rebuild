# Retain Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/retain-cards-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Retain users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Retain's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Use the date and grade they give you | +15–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | The flashcards from the ad, before the price | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Tracking, notifications, rating and account after the price | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Proof and the saving where people decide | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 05 | A way out of the paywall | +10–15% ARPU |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Retain's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Retain. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
